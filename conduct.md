The *Madagascar Workshop on Conservation Genetics and Lanscape Sustainability* is committed to providing a safe, caring and inclusive learning and working environment. The personal and professional conduct of all participants will reflect our collective principles of dignity, integrity, and respect for the law. The workshop has a zero tolerance policy with respect to personal or sexual harassment in any form. We promote respect and academic excellence by expecting all participants to:

* Respect the rights of others and treat one another with dignity and respect at all times, regardless of economic status, race, colour, national or ethnic origin, language group, religion, gender, age, or ability.
* Respect and comply with national laws and local rules.
* Demonstrate honesty and integrity.
* Take appropriate measures to help those in need.

If you have experienced or observed a violation of the code of conduct please communicate this to a member of the course faculty. They will ensure that appropriate action is taken.
